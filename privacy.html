Privacy

This page says what we collect, who can see it, and how you get rid of it. Everything written here matches what the application actually does.

Last updated: 2026-08-31

What we collect

From a driver: name, phone, city or area, languages spoken, nationality (optional), US work authorization, licences held, years of experience, the kinds of transport sought, availability, how you want employers to reach you, and — if you provide them — a link to your CV or a few lines about your experience.

From an employer: organisation, kind of business, contact person, phone, and what they are looking for.

We also record when you agreed to WhatsApp alerts, because a consent has to be datable to be worth anything. We do not ask for a date of birth, an address, a licence number or a social security number.

Who sees what

An employer browsing the pool sees no name, no phone, no nationality, no CV and no work-authorization status — only an anonymous reference, the licences, the years of experience, the town, the kinds of transport sought and the availability. If you came through Driver Coach and ticked the second box, they also see how far along you are: preparation, written-test score, exam results and test date. Without that box, none of it.

Those details appear only when the employer selects you, which costs them a contact and is final for them.

If you chose “an employer must have my permission”, they do not even get them then: your selection becomes a request, and nothing is sent until you agree from your own page.

Nationality is never in the browse list. US law forbids discriminating on national origin, and a field visible while sorting candidates is an invitation to do exactly that. It appears only after a selection, with the contact.

We are not a background-check agency

Driver360 is not a consumer reporting agency, and nothing it shows an employer is a consumer report within the meaning of the Fair Credit Reporting Act. We do not run background checks, we do not verify driving records, we do not check criminal history, and we do not contact anyone about you.

Everything an employer sees comes from what you typed yourself, or from a result you entered and chose to publish. We do not confirm it. An employer who wants a verified driving record must obtain it the lawful way — from the RMV, with your written authorisation, or through a licensed agency — and the obligations that come with it are theirs, not ours.

So do not use this site as a screening tool. It is an introduction, not a check.

WhatsApp alerts

You only receive them if you ticked the box, which is separate from joining the pool — you can be findable without being messaged. Two a week at most. Reply STOP and it ends.

Today those messages are written and sent by a person, by hand. We say so rather than let you imagine an automated system we do not yet have.

Where the data lives, and for how long

Your file is stored by Cloudflare, which hosts the site and its database. When you talk to the coach, what you write is sent to Anthropic, which produces the answer. Those two companies process the data on our behalf; nobody else does.

A file that is never reconfirmed leaves the pool after 45 days and expires on its own afterwards. You can also delete it at any time with your code: it is removed immediately and we keep no copy.

We do not sell data, we do not rent it, and there is no advertising tracker on this site.

What your browser keeps

Your chosen language and your access code are kept in your own browser so that you do not retype them. They stay on your device; we do not read them.

There is no advertising cookie and no third-party tracker on this site.

Your rights, and how to use them

With your code you can see everything we hold about you, correct it, pause it so no employer sees you, or delete it. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

You can withdraw the WhatsApp consent by unticking the box, or by replying STOP.

If an employer claims to have hired you and that is not true, you can put yourself back in the pool yourself: the driver always has the last word on their own status.
