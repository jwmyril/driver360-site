Before you even apply — what the RMV asks for
  • 21 or over, with a Class A, B, C or D licence held 3 continuous years.
  • A transportation company named on the form — name, address, phone, e-mail. You must already work there, or expect to.
  • CORI and SORI checks. The CORI form is two pages and travels with the application; a new one is required at every renewal.
  • The RMV medical form, completed by a medical doctor (MD or DO) licensed in Massachusetts. A nurse practitioner or physician assistant is not accepted, and the exam must be dated within 90 days of the application.
  • Vision: at least 120° combined peripheral field, and you must tell red, green and amber apart.
  • 2 hours of pre-service training with your employer — then the written exam.
  • $15.00 for a one-year certificate ($7.50 for six months if you are 70+, an insulin-dependent diabetic, or have had a hypoglycemic episode).
  • Coming from another state or country: a certified out-of-state driving record and criminal record, each dated within 90 days.
  • Originals only — no copies. The application is signed under the penalties of perjury.

Immigration status. Since 1 July 2023 the Work and Family Mobility Act lets Massachusetts residents obtain a Standard Class D licence regardless of immigration status, and the RMV's own 7D checklist lists no proof-of-lawful-presence document. We have not been able to confirm whether the 7D certificate itself adds one — so ask before you spend anything: RMV Vehicle Safety & Compliance Services, 857-368-8130.
