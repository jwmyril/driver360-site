What Driver360 is — and is not

Driver360 lists employers who hire drivers in Massachusetts, holds a pool where drivers can be found by those employers, and helps drivers prepare for the Class D road test and the 7D certificate exam.

We are not an employment agency and not a staffing firm. We do not employ drivers, we do not place them, and we take no commission on a hire. The contract, if there is one, is between the driver and the employer.

We are not affiliated with the Massachusetts RMV or with any employer listed on this site.

What is free and what is paid

Driver Pool is free for drivers, and stays free. Publishing a job opening is free for employers, and stays free.

Driver Employer costs $49 a month for 5 contacts, unused contacts rolling over one month, and $15 for an extra contact.

Driver Coach is free while we launch. It will become paid. Anyone who has access when that happens keeps it for the period they were given.

What we do not promise

We do not promise you a job, an interview, or that any employer will contact you. We do not promise you will pass an exam.

The job postings we publish come from employers. We check that the link works and that the employer exists; we do not verify pay, schedule or working conditions. Only the employer's own page binds the employer.

Driver Coach is independent preparation. During a driving session the phone stays in the hands of your supervising driver, vehicle stopped.

Your access code

Registering gives you a personal code. It is the only key to your file: it lets you change it, pause it, or delete it. There is no password and no e-mail account, which is deliberate — we ask for as little as possible.

Keep it. We cannot look it up for you automatically. If you lose it, write to us and we will check by hand who you are before doing anything.

Rules for employers

A selection is final and uses one contact. You see the pool without names before you spend anything, so that you choose knowingly.

Contact details are given to you so that you can offer that person work. Reselling them, adding them to a marketing list, or passing them to a third party ends your access immediately.

You must declare the outcome of your selections — hired or not selected. After three undeclared selections, selecting is blocked. It is what keeps the pool honest for the drivers.

If a driver asked to approve each employer, your selection sends them a request. You get their details only if they agree, and your contact is given back if they refuse.

Suspension, and changes to these terms

We can close an access that abuses the service — false information, harassment of a driver, reuse of contact details, or an attempt to extract the pool.

We may change these terms. The date at the top says when they were last changed. If a change matters to you and you disagree, delete your file: it takes one click and it is immediate.
