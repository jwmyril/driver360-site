Proposal · to discuss Driver360 · Atmart Massachusetts 16 September 2026 No Amazon integration assumed

A driver’s experience can continue across employers.

Portable professional records, shared experience, and a path back after a career break — a private network for participating DSPs and drivers. This is a concept to validate, not a product that already exists.

Why this matters to me

My DSP is closing. I plan to take a break to focus on Atmart. I would like to keep my professional experience, contribute while I am away, and return when I am ready.

A closure is the starting case. The need continues after the closure.

I am speaking from my own situation, not for every driver.

The gap we want to validate

Between employers
Where does a driver keep experience they can share again?
During a break
How can they stay connected without receiving job offers?
When returning
What can they reuse, and what must they complete again?

These are discovery questions, not claims about Amazon’s current systems. Some of this may already exist — that is the first thing I would like to learn.

What we propose

Professional record

Roles and employment dates, vehicle and delivery experience, training evidence and references — kept by the driver and reusable.

The driver chooses the recipient and the scope of each share. A new employer still performs its own required checks.
Experience exchange

Reviewed lessons that help other drivers: preparing for a first week, changing roles, questions from returning drivers.

Voluntary, and reviewed by a moderator before publication. No customer addresses, delivery photos, route data or confidential information. No public rating of colleagues. Employer policy guides operations.
Pause and return

Driver-controlled availability — job outreach off during a break — and a checklist for returning.

Proposed access through participating DSPs. The governance still requires validation.

How trust becomes visible

LevelWhat it means
Driver statementWhat the driver says about their own experience.
Supporting documentA document the driver attaches to a statement.
Named confirmationA specific fact confirmed by an identified employer, with a date.

Each piece of information keeps its source and its date. No reliability score, no ranking of people, no automatic access to Amazon data.

A clearer decision for drivers and DSPs

Driver view

Pay and stated hours · location and acceptable commute · requirements still to complete.

DSP view

Interest in this specific opening · experience shared with permission · evidence status and next steps.

The driver chooses; the DSP decides. The pilot would test usefulness, successful returns and continued employment — we make no retention promise before measuring it.

Trust and access

Driver control

Specific recipients, optional contributions and outreach preferences. Access can be paused, and deletion requested.

Accountability

Dated evidence, corrections, and a record of who accessed what.

DSP responsibility

Hiring decisions and required checks remain with each employer.

No Amazon integration or endorsement is assumed. No shared driver blacklist. Any future access to Amazon data would require separate authorization.

Revoking future access cannot erase copies an employer has already exported: retention and dispute rules have to be set before any pilot. These are design choices, not a legal conclusion.

A focused pilot

First 30 days, proposed

Agree the minimum record · test sharing and pause preferences · collect driver and DSP feedback.

When drivers return

Which information was reusable? · Which checks had to restart? · Did the new employment continue?

Who

Volunteers from the affected group — drivers who want work now and drivers taking a break — and one or two participating DSPs, subject to agreement.

Size, duration and sponsor to be agreed. Returns are followed when they actually happen, not squeezed into 30 days.
Where Driver360 stands today · 15 September 2026

Live at driver360.atmart.ltd: a driver pool, a portal for DSPs, a link to Amazon’s official page for DSP driver openings, and a career coach for drivers.

The pool is still empty — nobody has registered yet. Everything in a profile is what the driver declares; only the phone number is confirmed. Five drivers from the affected group have told me they want to take part; I have not asked anyone to register until we know the concept is worth testing.

Not built yet: the portable record and explicit sharing, the pause status and return checklist, and moderated contributions. Amazon integration is not part of any approved scope.
