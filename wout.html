Before you drive a single metre — what sends people home
  • Arrive about 15 minutes early. Late means no test.
  • A completed road test application — with the parental consent section filled in if you are under 18 — and your learner's permit.
  • A qualified sponsor: 21 or over, one year of driving experience, and a valid licence from their own US state. A foreign licence is not eligible. No sponsor, no test.
  • A vehicle that passes the examiner's inspection: registered, inspected, safe, with a clean and adequate seat for the examiner and a rear seat for your sponsor.
  • An accessible parking brake — the examiner must be able to stop the car. A centrally located one is safest; some minivans are rejected for this alone.
  • No donut spare tire. No repair plate. No dealer plate unless you are the dealer, their spouse, or a salesperson there.
  • Back-up camera, parking sensors, automatic parallel park, GPS-guided driving: turned off.
  • Out-of-state plates: proof of insurance at Massachusetts minimums ($20,000/$40,000 bodily injury, $5,000 property). A rental also needs the rental agreement and a letter from the company authorising a road test.
  • No children, no pets. Service animals are allowed. Only you, the examiner and your sponsor are in the car, and you may not talk to your sponsor unless the examiner allows it.
  • An interpreter is not a right. The manual says one may be allowed “if the examiner so authorizes”. Ask when you book — do not assume.
  • Under 18: a clean driving record for the six months before you apply, and it must stay clean until test day.
  • Six road tests maximum in 12 months. The $35 is charged again if you fail, are unprepared, are refused over the vehicle, bring no sponsor, are late or absent, or cancel with less than 72 hours notice.

Questions people actually ask

Can I bring an interpreter to the road test?
Not as a right. The manual says an interpreter “may also be allowed” if the examiner so authorizes. Ask when you book your test, and do not assume — drill the English commands daily so you are not depending on it.
Who can be my sponsor?
Someone 21 or over, with at least one year of driving experience, holding a valid licence from their own US state. A foreign licence is not eligible — the manual is explicit. Without a sponsor you are not given a Class D road test.
What can stop the test before it even starts?
Arriving late. An incomplete road-test application, or no parental consent if you are under 18. A vehicle the examiner rejects: parking brake not reachable, a “donut” spare tire, a repair plate, or driver aids that cannot be turned off. Children or pets in the car — service animals are allowed.
How many times can I take the road test?
No more than six in a 12-month period for a Class D licence. The $35 fee is charged again if you fail, are unprepared, are refused over the vehicle, come without a sponsor, are late or absent, or cancel with less than 72 hours notice.
Does Driver Coach guarantee I will pass?
No. It is independent preparation and it is not affiliated with the RMV. The RMV publishes no scoring sheet, so nobody can tell you exactly what the examiner counts — our fault grid is ours, and we say so on every screen where it appears.

🎥 Film yourself to see yourself

The video stays on your phone. It goes nowhere, we never see it, and there is no upload: it disappears when you close the page. And we do not score your face, your posture or your “confidence”. Those signals differ from one culture to the next: scoring them would mean measuring how far you sit from a norm. The camera is a mirror, not a jury.
