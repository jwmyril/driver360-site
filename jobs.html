What these jobs pay

School bus driver posts in Massachusetts were advertised at roughly $27 to $32 an hour in August 2026, and CDL routes often carry a sign-on bonus. Those are figures read on job boards, not a promise from anyone: the only number that binds an employer is the one on the employer's own page. Many of these posts are split shifts — morning and afternoon — which suits people who need the middle of the day free.
